# Overnight oats

A make-ahead breakfast that takes five minutes the night before.

## Ingredients

- 50 g rolled oats
- 150 ml milk or oat milk
- 1 tbsp chia seeds
- 1 tsp maple syrup
- Toppings: fresh fruit, nut butter, granola

## Method

1. Stir everything except the toppings in a jar.
2. Refrigerate overnight.
3. Top in the morning and eat cold.
