# Links and clicks

Try each on a rendered line (cursor elsewhere) and on a revealed line (cursor on it).

- Plain click on [[01-arrows-bullet-heading]]: opens the note when rendered.
- Cmd+click (macOS) / Ctrl+click (Windows/Linux) on [[02-arrows-with-scroll]]: opens the note even when the line is revealed.
- Ctrl+click on macOS on [[03-inline-same-line]]: does not navigate.
- Right click on [[04-blocks-reveal]]: does not navigate.
- Plain click on [an external link](https://example.com): places the cursor.
- Cmd/Ctrl+click on [an external link](https://example.com): opens the browser.
- Bare URL: https://example.com and autolink <https://example.com>

Switch to source mode and back: wikilinks and callouts are colored in source mode and not styled twice in live preview.
