# Scout 20: themes repo (26 themes) against READABILITY_PAIRS and extra pairs

Read-only. Scripts and raw output: `/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-diegorv-Dev-pet-projects-koko-brain/d34954df-1fbf-4743-96fe-27db3527c128/scratchpad/audit-20-themes-repo/` (builder.mts = patched copy of theme-builder.logic.ts, build.mts, pairs.mts, extra.mts, sim.mts, fgon.mts, apca.mts, dark.mts, zet.mts, hyg.mts, fmt.mts, inp.mts; outputs pairs.out, extra.out, sim.out). ## Method

1. Built every theme file in `koko-brain-themes/themes/*.json` (26) through a copy of `buildTheme` (`palette` + `overrides`), in three modes: `full` (what the app ships), `raw` (no `readableCore`, no final pass), and the app path. The `full` output equals `theme-tokens.json` token for token on all 26 (0 mismatches), so `theme-tokens.json` is the shipped result. `index.json` equals the per-file content for all 26 (in sync). `color.mts` is byte-identical to `src/lib/utils/color.ts`.
2. Ran `READABILITY_PAIRS` (the exact export) on `full` and `raw`. Backgrounds flattened over `editorBg` as `withReadableContrast` does.
3. Ran 150+ extra pairs the app relies on (muted/primary/error/warning/success text on hover, selected, accent, setting, input, embed, collection surfaces; translucent editor overlays; non-text borders, rings, checkboxes) on all 28 themes (26 + 2 built-ins from `theme-tokens.json`), WCAG 2 ratio. Tints composited in sRGB alpha over the real ancestor.
4. APCA Lc for every 4.5-floor text pair of `READABILITY_PAIRS` (formula copied from `color.ts`, 0.0.98G constants).
5. Simulated builder fixes with `ensureContrast` (mutedFg, primary, error lifted on the extra surfaces) to decide palette vs derivation.
6. Dark-mode detection: `isDarkColor(editorBg)` vs `dark` flag vs fg/bg polarity vs every surface token.

## Result of READABILITY_PAIRS

- `full`: 0 failures on 26/26 themes. The pipeline guarantees every listed pair by construction (final pass moves the foreground only, never the background).
- `raw` (palette+overrides as the repo authors wrote them): 318 pair failures; worst palettes: Zettlr Berlin Light 27 distinct fg tokens, Zettlr Bielefeld Light 27, Zettlr Bordeaux Light 26, Frankfurt Dark 23, Sakura 21, KMS Dark 19. 8 themes need no lift at all (Cool Mist, Deep Ocean, Foolscap, Graphite Violet, Ink Night, Lumen Contrast, Sepia, Tolaria Dark); Mint Linen and Shimmering Focus Dark need one token. So the repo values are not the readable result; the app rewrites them. Core fields rewritten by `readableCore`: Sakura 7, Zettlr Berlin/Bielefeld/Bordeaux Light 7 each, SF Light 6, Frankfurt Dark 6, KMS Dark 5, Willemstad Dark 4. Sakura additionally has 9 overrides altered by the final pass (e.g. `preview.wikilink #8860B0 -> #865fae`). This is by design (README says the app raises contrast) and is not a readability defect.
- Hygiene, no readability effect: Sakura and Twilight Violet carry `preview.frontmatterBg` in `overrides.preview`, a retired token (`theme-validation.logic.ts:43`); the app strips it at parse. Every other override key exists in the current token set.

The failures that remain are all outside `READABILITY_PAIRS`. Where they come from is below.

## Findings (also returned structured)

### F1. primaryForeground / destructiveForeground flip to near-black at APCA Lc 33-41 (app, with palette cause)
`theme-builder.logic.ts:186-198`. `readableCore` first lifts `primary` (and `error`) for use as text on the editor background, then `ensureContrast(primaryFg, primary, 4.5)` only nudges the author's foreground to the WCAG floor on whichever side of the fill it already was, or flips to the other extreme when the author's polarity can no longer reach 4.5. The result passes WCAG at 4.5 to 4.6 but reads poorly, the exact black-on-saturated-mid-tone problem.
- primaryForeground on primary, Lc under 45: Zettlr Berlin Light `#030305` on `#12865e` 4.51, Lc33 (raw `#282832` on `#1cb27e`; white on the lifted fill is 4.57, Lc77); Willemstad Dark `#090909` on `#567f83` 4.51, Lc33 (raw white on `#0d5257` was 8.89; the fill was lifted to be readable as link text, white is then 4.41, Lc76); Twilight Violet `#1a181f` on `#9a7bb9` Lc39 (white Lc69); Zettlr Frankfurt Dark `#2b2b2b` on `#5e97c7` Lc41 (white Lc64); Zettlr KMS Dark `#2b2b2b` on `#eb6a60` Lc41 (white Lc63). Zettlr Berlin Dark Lc47 (white Lc57). Used by every default Button (`button.svelte:10`), calendar today cells, selected tabs, Kanban chips, meta-bind primary buttons (`app.css:455`).
- destructiveForeground on destructive, Lc under 45: Shimmering Focus Light `#0f0f0f` on `#e93147` Lc36, Willemstad Light Lc36, Tolaria Light `#121212` on `#e53e3e` Lc36, Twilight Violet Lc37, Shimmering Focus Dark and Willemstad Dark `#222222` on `#fb464c` Lc40. White would be Lc66-73 on all six (WCAG 3.45-4.20, which is why the lift flipped to black). The shadcn destructive Button hardcodes `text-white` and ignores this token (scout 04 F3); the token is read by the meta-bind destructive button (`app.css:459`) and by mods, so the visible cases are meta-bind buttons and mod themes.
Fix direction: choose the label polarity by APCA (as `labelColors` does) and move the fill, not the text, to reach 4.5; or keep the author's polarity when it already has Lc 60+ and lift the fill. Willemstad Dark and the Zettlr Frankfurt/KMS Dark palettes need a separate button fill or a darker/lighter `primary` because one token serves as fill and as text on the dark editor.

### F2. readableCore lifts text to exactly 4.5 on four plain surfaces, so state surfaces from the same palette fail (app)
`theme-builder.logic.ts:183` (`surfaces = [editorBg, sidebarBg, panelBg, popoverBg]`), `:186` (`primary` lifted on editorBg only), `:187` (`error` lifted to 3), `:192` (`mutedFg` on the four surfaces). The lifted values land at 4.50 to 4.55 on the lightest-contrast surface, so any surface lighter than the editor in a dark theme (darker in a light theme) fails: `hover` (`ui.muted`, `settingsHoverBg`), `selected` (`ui.accent`, `fileExplorerAccent`, `rightSidebarAccent`), tints of the same fg.
Measured on the shipped themes (worst per pair):

- primary as text on `muted`/`accent`/`fileExplorerAccent`/`rightSidebarAccent`: 16/28 under 4.5: Zettlr Frankfurt Dark 2.58, KMS Dark 2.60, Berlin Light 2.73, Bordeaux Light 2.80, KMS Light 2.82, Frankfurt Light 2.95, Berlin Dark 2.96, Bielefeld Light 3.03, Twilight Violet 3.41, SF Light 3.37, Sakura 3.52, Graphite Violet 3.58.
- error as text (`text-destructive`) on `background`/`card`/`popover`/`settingItemBg`/`inputBg`/`muted`/`accent`/`editorBg`: 20/28 fail; the Zettlr Dark family reaches 1.64-1.72 on the hover surface (Bordeaux Dark 1.64), 3.01 on editorBg (KMS Dark). `error` is lifted to 3.0 only.
Simulation (`sim.out`): lifting each of these on the extra surfaces is reachable in all 28 themes (0 infeasible) but moves tokens a long way: Zettlr Dark mutedFg `#91919a -> #c2c2c7` (7.97 vs fg 12.41), Zettlr Bordeaux Dark muted lands at 8.46 vs fg 8.97 and Zettlr Light mutedFg at 7.7 vs fg 7.97, so the secondary text hierarchy disappears. Zettlr Berlin Dark primary `#1cb27e -> #8ad1af`. So a builder-only fix is wrong for the Zettlr family; see F3.
Cross-reference: scout 01 findings 6, 7, 8, 9, 10 and scout 12 item 5 measured the app usages (same numbers); this finding is the derivation root cause.

### F3. Zettlr palettes use the border gray as the hover and selected surface (themes repo, needs F2 in the app)
`themes/zettlr-*-dark.json` palettes: `hover #505050` (equal to `border` and `borderStrong`) on editor `#2b2b2c` (Bordeaux `#002b36`); `selected #282832`. `themes/zettlr-*-light.json`: `hover #dcdcdc`, `selected #c8c8c8` (equal to `borderStrong`) on editor `#ffffff`. `mutedFg #787882` (lifted to `#91919a`/`#6e6e77` by the app).
- Dark x5: fg `#f0f0f0` on hover is only 4.82 (Bordeaux Dark 4.82), muted 2.52-2.58, primary 2.58-2.96, error 1.64-1.72. The lifted muted text (`#91919a`) reaches 4.5 only on surfaces at or below `#2b2b2b`, i.e. on no visible hover fill.
- Light x5: fg `#50505a` on selected is 4.76, primary on selected 2.73-3.03, muted 3.02 (needs a surface at or above `#f2f2f2`, the editor itself).
Fix direction in the repo: hover and selected fills closer to the page (indicative, not measured: dark about `#383838`, light about `#ebebeb`) and a lighter (dark) / darker (light) `mutedFg` in the palette so the app's lift has headroom; a builder-only fix (F2) flattens the muted/fg hierarchy in these 10 themes.

### F4. mutedForeground family at APCA Lc 38-42 on 6 dark themes (both)
All 4.5 pairs pass WCAG at 4.50 to 4.53, but APCA is under 45 on Zettlr Dark x5 and Twilight Violet: `mutedForeground` on editorBg Lc39 (Bordeaux Dark `#8f8f98` on `#002b36`, Twilight Violet `#908aa4` on `#222028`), on card Lc38-39, on background Lc39-42; derived tokens at the same value: `fileExplorerMutedFg`/`BadgeFg` Lc40-42, `rightSidebarMutedFg` Lc38-42, `statusBarFg` Lc38-42, `tabTextInactive` Lc41-42, `syntax.quote` Lc39, `collectionHeader`/`collectionLoading` Lc41-42. ### F5. Links, wikilinks, footnotes and URLs at Lc 31-44 on dark themes (both)
`syntax.link`/`url`, `preview.link`/`footnote`, `wikilink.target`/`display` pass 4.5 but read poorly: Willemstad Dark `#567f83` on `#070707` 4.57, Lc31 (worst); Zettlr Frankfurt Dark `#5e97c7` Lc40, KMS Dark `#eb6a60` Lc40, Twilight Violet wikilink `#9a7bb9` Lc36 and link `#7898c8` Lc44, Bordeaux Dark `wikilink.heading #e3685e` Lc39. Links are body-size text in the editor. Willemstad Dark palette `primary #0d5257` is a dark teal button fill that is 2.4:1 on its own near-black editor; `readableCore` lifts it only to the WCAG floor, which gives Lc31. Fix: palette (give Willemstad Dark a lighter link/primary, or use `linkFg`), plus an APCA target for accent text on dark in the app.

### F6. Color syntaxes the schema accepts but color.ts cannot parse (both, latent)
The themes-repo schema (`schema/theme.schema.json:593`), its README and the app validator (`theme-validation.logic.ts:8-9`) accept `hsl()/hsla()`, 4-digit hex and space-separated `rgb(255 255 255 / 50%)`. `parseRgba` (`color.ts:14`) handles none of them. Measured with `isDarkColor`, `contrastRatio`, `ensureContrast` (`fmt.mts`): `hsl(0 0% 100%)`, `hsl(0, 0%, 100%)`, `hsla(...)`, `#ffff`, `rgb(255 255 255)`, `rgb(255 255 255 / 50%)` all return `null`/unchanged. Consequences for a theme using one: (a) a light editor background in `hsl()` counts as dark (`theme.service.ts:31`, `theme.logic.ts:41`), so `html.dark`, `color-scheme: dark` and black shadows are applied on a light page; (b) any text token in those syntaxes skips every lift (`ensureContrast` returns the input), so `READABILITY_PAIRS` is silently not enforced. None of the 26 themes uses them (all hex or `rgba(r,g,b,a)`), so nothing is broken today. Fix: teach `parseRgba` hsl and the other forms, or drop them from the schema.

### F7. Input and Select borders are not distinguishable from the surface (both)
`components/ui/input/input.svelte:28,43` and `select-trigger.svelte`: `border-input` (= `borderStrong`) around `bg-input-bg` (= `panelBg`). Border against the dialog/row it sits on (`settingsDialogBg`, `settingItemBg`): 26/28 under 3:1; worst Twilight Violet 1.17, Sakura 1.18 / 1.06, KokoBrain Default 1.21 / 1.09, Lavender Haze 1.23 / 1.08, Tolaria Light 1.41 / 1.31; only Lumen Contrast (4.29) and Willemstad Light (3.72) pass. The fill is also near the surface (`inputBg` vs `settingItemBg` 1.04 to 1.5), so the field boundary relies on the border. Typed text (`text-foreground/80` on `inputBg`) is fine: 4.70 to 12.07. `borderStrong` is the same token as the unchecked switch track (scout 01 finding 5). `READABILITY_PAIRS` has no non-text pair. Fix: lift `input` to 3:1 on the surface it sits on in `buildTheme`, and raise `borderStrong` in the repo palettes where it equals the surface gray (Sakura, Twilight Violet, Zettlr Dark, KokoBrain Default, Lavender Haze).

## Dark-mode detection

`html.dark` = `isDarkColor(editorBg) !== false` (`theme.service.ts:31`), `--elev-*` and `appliedThemeStore` use the same. For all 28 themes the detected value equals the `dark` flag (13 dark, 15 light); editor luminance is at most 0.028 for dark and at least 0.842 for light (threshold 0.2), no theme is near the boundary; fg is lighter than editorBg exactly on the dark themes (polarity ok); no ui surface token (background, card, popover, fileExplorerBg, rightSidebarBg, statusBarBg, tabBar, settings*, inputBg, editorEmptyBg, muted, accent) has the opposite polarity of the editor. Names containing "light" match only Twilight Violet by accident (dark theme). The only inconsistency path is F6 (unparseable editorBg counts as dark).
