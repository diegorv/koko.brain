# Review: synthesis-draft.md, lens = incremental sequencing and scope

Reviewer stance: I assumed the plan is over-engineered and badly ordered, and tried to prove it. Repo at `main` 2a937e57. Read-only. Nothing compiled or run. Code claims were checked by reading files (path:line). Anything not checked is marked UNVERIFIED.

## 0. Verdict

The A-then-B direction holds. The sequencing does not. The first thing the owner can use on a phone (M3, iPad read-only) sits behind about 6 weeks of work (roll-up, `synthesis-draft.md:321-324`), and most of that time goes to seams that neither phase needs (M1) and to a desktop behaviour change that read-only access does not need (M2). There is a cheaper path to the first useful build. It reuses a mechanism that already exists in the installed Tauri JS API and that the E2E suite already relies on in another form, and it puts iPad read-only at about 2-2.5 weeks (estimate). Two findings would break desktop behaviour as written (F4, F6).

## 2. Cheaper sequencing (proposal)

Sizes are estimates. A sub-milestone counts as "shippable" only if it can be merged on its own and leaves desktop green.

| Step | Content (draft task ids) | Phone value at exit | Desktop change | Est. |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| M0 spike | S1 route, S4 keyboard, S5 persistence; S2 replaced by "the shim boots the real bundle" (F1) | go/no-go | none (throwaway) | S |
| M1' host on loopback | 3.1, 3.3, 3.4 (read + Virtual list incl. F6), 3.5 hub, read-only `plugin:fs\|*` + VaultScope in the dispatcher (F2), `ipc-shim.ts` (F1), lifecycle reduced (F7), settings no-save | **the app reads the real vault in Mac Safari at 127.0.0.1** | opt-in server, off by default | M |

Effect, as an estimate: iPad read moves from ~6 weeks to ~2-2.5 weeks, and phone editing from ~9.5 weeks to ~6-7 weeks. M1 (6-8 days) is removed and M2 shrinks from M to about S-M. The owner decision Q1 is only needed before phone editing, not before read-only.

What this gives up relative to the draft: (a) typed seams in `core/`, which a later headless `kokobrain serve` (M10) or a Rust-to-TS move would want. They can still be added then, as a refactor and not a prerequisite. This repeats design A's own position (`design-A-remote.md:240`). (b) Atomic Rust CAS in phase 1. The race in F3 has to be accepted explicitly by the owner, or the Rust CAS kept in M4'.
