## Findings

1. **blocker** `src/lib/plugins/kanban/kanban.logic.ts:226` (with moveItem :377, archiveItem :469, archiveCompletedItems :487, restoreItem :506). Every raw line attaches to the card before it and moves with that card, so a move, reorder, archive or restore takes lines out of their place. The Issue asks for the opposite ("The cards under a `### Sub` line stay under it", raw lines "re-emitted at the same position"). What my probes saw:
   - (a) A `### Sub` heading or a prose paragraph leaves its lane, or lands after the wrong cards, when the card above it is dragged or archived. This is the not-met criterion above.
   - (b) Restoring an archived card whose raw lines include a `## X` line (headings after `## Archive` are raw by design) moves that line into the active area. `## A\n\n- [ ] a1\n\n---\n\n## Archive\n\n- [x] old\n## Later\n- [x] older\n` + restore `old` writes a new lane `Later` on reopen. A card action creates a lane.
   - (c) Moving a card that has a raw `---` after it, to just above a lane titled `Archive` (addLane can make one), writes `---`, blank, `## Archive`, which is the archive form. On reopen, lanes `Archive` and `C` turn into archived cards: `## A\n\n- [ ] a\n---\n\n## B\n\n- [ ] b\n\n## Archive\n\n- [ ] keep\n\n## C\n\n- [ ] c\n`, then move `a` to the end of B, gives archive = `["keep","c"]`.

   Fix: when a card leaves its position (moveItem both branches, archiveItem, archiveCompletedItems, restoreItem), hand its raw lines to the previous card or the lane / archive head, the way removeItem already does. That leaves them in place. If continuation lines should travel, carry only the indented ones (leading whitespace) with the card. Add tests for: a move and a same-lane reorder of the card above `### Sub` (cards after Sub still follow it); restore of an archive card followed by a `## X` raw line (no new lane); and the `---` + `Archive` lane case. Then update 'moving a card carries the raw lines attached to it' (`kanban.logic.test.ts:1752`) to the chosen model.
2. **minor** `src/lib/plugins/kanban/kanban.logic.ts:112-142` (extractSettingsBlock). With an unclosed `%% kanban:settings` block, a settings change appends a fresh block. On the next parse, `SETTINGS_CLOSE_RE` (`/^%%/`) matches the second block's opening line as the close. My probe: setViewMode('board') and then setViewMode('list') on `...\n%% kanban:settings\n{"viewMode":"list"}\n` leave `"viewMode"` stuck at the first value. Each change also reorders the settings lines further (`{\n  "viewMode": "board"\n}\n%%\n%% kanban:settings\n{"viewMode":"list"}\n\n%% kanban:settings\n`). Fix: do not treat a line that matches `SETTINGS_OPEN_RE` as a close, or keep an unclosed opener as a raw line and look for the next opener.
3. **minor** `src/lib/plugins/kanban/kanban.logic.ts:20`. `SETTINGS_OPEN_RE` allows leading whitespace and is searched over the whole file, so an indented `%% kanban:settings` line inside a fenced code block that a card's raw lines carry is pulled out and moved to the end of the file. The code fence is split: probe output `- [x] a\n```\n```\n  %% kanban:settings\n%%\n`. Fix: anchor the opener at column 0 (`^%%\s*kanban:settings\s*$`, which is what the serializer writes), or skip fenced regions.
4. **minor** `src/lib/plugins/kanban/kanban.logic.ts:88` (frontmatterEnd). A frontmatter block with a `## ` YAML comment (`---\n## comment\ntags: [x]\n---\n\n## A...`) is not detected, so `## comment` shows as a lane and its body holds the rest of the YAML. The bytes survive a toggle, but adding a card to that lane writes it inside the frontmatter. Fix: when line 0 is `---` and a closing fence exists before the first `- [ ]` card or archive form, treat it as frontmatter. Or accept the case and document it in discovery.md.
5. **nit** `src/lib/plugins/kanban/kanban.logic.ts:262`. When the archive is emptied by restore, the archive `gapLines` are dropped along with the section, so the file loses its trailing newline. Probe: `## A\n\n---\n\n## Archive\n\n- [x] old\n  detail\n` + restore gives `## A\n\n- [ ] old\n  detail`, with no final `\n`. Cosmetic, no data lost.

## Resolution

All five findings fixed in 8d1152c1 (one commit: the fixes share `kanban.logic.ts` and its test file).

1. The old 'moving a card carries the raw lines' test now asserts the indented line travels and a prose line stays.
2. Fixed. extractSettingsBlock restarts at a later opener, so an unclosed opener stays a raw line. Test 'an unclosed settings block stays a raw line and later setting changes stick' (two setViewMode rounds, stable output).
3. Fixed. `SETTINGS_OPEN_RE` is anchored at column 0. Test 'an indented settings opener (inside a code fence) is a raw line'. A column-0 opener inside a fence is still read as the block.
4. Fixed. frontmatterEnd stops at a card line, and rejects a fence only when the lines before it are lane headings and blanks alone, or the fence opens the archive. Tests 'a frontmatter with a "## " YAML comment is still frontmatter' (addItem lands in lane A, outside the frontmatter) and 'a "---" on line 0, an empty lane, then the archive is a rule'. Residual: `---` on line 0, a lane heading, a prose line, then a lone `---` (no cards before it) now reads as frontmatter.
5. Fixed. serializeKanbanBoard writes a final blank line when it drops an archive that had gap lines. Test 'emptying the archive by restore keeps the trailing newline'.
