# Composer tag and wikilink autocomplete

The Quick Capture composer popover (`src/routes/composer/+page.svelte`) gets `#tag` and `[[wikilink]]` completion that behaves like the editor's. The editor's two completion sources are reused unchanged inside a minimal CodeMirror view. Editor behavior must not change: no file under `src/lib/core/markdown-editor/`, `src/lib/app/editor-host.ts` or `src/lib/app/EditorView.svelte` is edited.

Design: `research/design.md`. Adversarial pass: `research/design-refute.md` (its M1, S1, S3, S4, N4 fixes are folded into the tasks below).
