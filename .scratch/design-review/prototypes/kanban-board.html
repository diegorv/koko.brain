Clear improvements

P02 Cards can move between lanes while a sort or filter is active clearinteractioneffort M

With a sort or filter on, canReorder is false and every card turns undraggable with no explanation and no menu or keyboard alternative.

  • Turn on a sort or the filter, then drag a card to another lane. After: it lands at the end of that lane with a labelled bar. A drop in the same lane does nothing and the lane shows a no-drop outline.
  • Right-click a card, then Move to. In manual order, grab the grip in a lane header: a vertical insertion bar shows the target position. The lane grip stays hidden while sorted or filtered, as today.
  • Escape is untouched: native drag already cancels on it.

P03 Keyboard shortcuts act on the card you can see clearaccessibilityeffort M

One root handler uses a hidden "virtual" focus over the unfiltered lanes and runs in board, list and table modes. A filtered-out card can be deleted by Backspace.

  • Before: filter by #design, press ArrowDown twice, then Backspace. The ring sits on a visible card while the deleted one is not on screen (log line shows it). Switch to List and press Backspace: a card nobody can see is gone.
  • After: Tab lands on one card (roving tabindex). Arrows move among visible cards only. Space and Backspace act only when the card itself is focused, so Space on a check button just toggles that button. In List or Table the keys do nothing. Changing the filter or mode resets focus.

P04 Renaming a lane keeps its settings, new lanes get unique names clearfeedbackeffort S

Lane settings are keyed by title. Rename "Done" and auto-complete, the card limit and collapsed state are orphaned. Every new lane is "New Lane".

  • The JSON strip under each board is the lane settings block saved in the file. Orphaned keys turn red, migrated keys turn green. Renaming to an existing title suffixes " 2". Add lane twice to see "New Lane", "New Lane 2".

P05 Adding a card and lane actions are always visible clearinteractioneffort M

The add-card form is opacity 0 inside the scrolling list, empty lanes are blank, lane actions hide in a right-click menu, header indicators have no labels.

  • Use the buttons to fill Backlog until it scrolls: the "+ Add card" row stays pinned under the list. Hover or Tab onto a lane header for the "..." menu. Hover the green check and the count for tooltips.

Moderate improvements

P06 Wide boards: edge fades, toolbar actions, a collapsed lane is a rail moderatelayouteffort M

At 1440 px three lanes fit, the 4th is cut off with no cue, Add lane and Archive sit after the last lane, a collapsed lane keeps its full width.

  • Collapse a lane with its chevron: before it still takes 272 px, after it becomes a rail that accepts drops (drag a card onto it). Add lane in the toolbar scrolls the new lane into view. Archive (n) opens a panel under the toolbar button.

P143 The filter clears, explains and stays with its board moderatefeedbackeffort S

No clear button, Escape does nothing, counts show filtered numbers, emptied lanes look broken, and the query follows you to the next board.

  • Matching stays on raw text, so #tag and 2026-11 date fragments still work. Type "design", then Load the other board: before keeps the query and shows empty lanes, after resets it.

P145 Inline card edit grows and hides metadata tokens moderateinteractioneffort S

The edit box is one fixed row and shows the raw {date} and {color:x} tokens; focus falls to the page after editing.

  • Enter saves, Escape cancels. After saving, the strip shows the text stored in the file: the date and colour tokens are still there although you never saw them.

P88 Click a tag to filter; set tag colour from the card menu moderateaccessibilityeffort S

A tag chip opens a colour palette (mouse only, mousedown, no keyboard path) while the board already has a filter box.

  • The shared swatch group below is what P88 promotes for canvas, tags and kanban: named radios, selection shown by a check and not only a ring, a real focus ring, arrow keys inside the group.
