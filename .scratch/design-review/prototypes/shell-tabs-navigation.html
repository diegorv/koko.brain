Clear wins
P34

The tab strip has no overflow cue or control, fixed 160 px titles, and no reorder

clear

With five or more tabs the strip scrolls silently: the edge tab is clipped, there is no list of tabs, tabs never shrink, cannot be reordered and have no tooltip.

Before real app, 5 tabs open
5th tab clipped, no cue
After flex 220 to 100 px, edge fades, list of tabs, drag marker, full-path tooltip, wheel scrolls sideways

Try it: open tabs until they shrink to 100 px and the strip starts to scroll (edge fades appear, the chevron shows a hidden count), drag a tab to reorder (pinned tabs stay first), hover a tab for the full path, scroll the wheel over the strip, open the list with the chevron.

Owner decision: should a plain click replace the active unpinned tab instead of adding one? Cmd+click, middle-click and "Open in new tab" always add a tab. Switch the policy below. Downside of Replace: a large behavior change (a tab you meant to keep gets overwritten, though Back undoes it). Alternative: keep today's behavior and only rename "Open in new tab" to "Open", relying on P34 for tab sprawl.
