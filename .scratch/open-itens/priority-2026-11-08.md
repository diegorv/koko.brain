# Open Issues: priority order (2026-11-08)

Every open Issue from the feature folders below, moved here and numbered in priority order: data safety and real bugs first, cosmetic polish last. `/relay-lite:run open-itens` takes the lowest available number, so the numbers are the order. `.scratch/mods-run-cancellation/` was left in place (its Issue 02 was being delivered).

Origin folders: `audit-2026-11-05`, `tasks-view`, `view-engine-fixes`, `view-editor`, `usage-telemetry`, `vault-mods`, `mods-observability`, `rail-styles`, `ios-app`, `inline-title`, `semantic-update-queue`. Their PRDs, raw evidence and closed Issues are in `.scratch/archived/<feature>/` (`inline-title` and `semantic-update-queue` held only their Issue, so they have no folder left). Each Issue file names its origin on its **Origin:** line; bare Issue numbers inside an Issue's text use its origin feature's numbering, translated by the map at the end of this file.

## How the order was built

- Each open Issue was classified from its text (class, impact, likelihood, effort, cosmetic or not), then ranked. An opus review tried to refute the draft and checked the Phase 1 bugs against `main` at `9ad851775`: none was fixed since the audit's own check at `5f8003d67`.
- The audit's first-wave order (`.scratch/archived/audit-2026-11-05/PRD.md`, § First wave) is kept, except that its polish runs (themes, tokens, settings layout, docs) move to Phase 6.
- A blocker always gets a lower number than the Issues it blocks. Five Issues were pulled forward that way: VD-06 for VD-07, VE-06, VE-13 and VE-15 for VE-08, and audit 68 for audit 142.
- Blockers that were already closed in their origin folder were dropped; the Issue's **Blocked by (origin):** line records them, and any note the original line carried.
- Four waits stated only in the text became real blockers: tasks-view 16 on audit 115 (030), tasks-view 21 and 22 on audit 116 (031), tasks-view 09 on audit 134 (085).
- Classification came from the Issue text. Only Phase 1 was checked against the code, and no repro test was run.
