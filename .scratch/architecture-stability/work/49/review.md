## Findings

1. **blocker**, `src/lib/features/event-calendar/event-calendar.service.ts:615` and `:1145`: `gone` treats every outside delete of `restored` recorded during the restore as a delete of the restored note. That includes a delete of another note X at the same path whose consumer call lands after the restore started, while the delete's disk move landed before `restoreItem`'s `exists(restorePath)`. In that case `restoreItem` restores to the original path, `gone` is true, and `restoreNow` puts a settled `delete` tombstone over a note that is on disk. It also drops the note's overlay (and its owned ops on a suffixed restore). This is the symptom the Issue's title names, a tombstone over the restored note for two bumps, in an interleaving where main behaves correctly. The probe above (outsideCreate Standup, refresh, hold, outsideDelete Standup, flush 60, holdRename, restore Standup, flush 60, release, flush 60, release, settle, refresh, check) passes on main and fails on the branch. The window is the same order as the race being fixed: the outside delete's `saveManifest` plus a few awaits. discovery.md records it as a "known risk", but this trades a narrow bug for a sibling one that main did not have. **Fix:** decide `gone` from the disk as well as the log. Keep `deletedOutside` registered through an `await exists(restored)` after `restoreItem` returns, and set `gone = matched && !(await exists(restored))`. A delete recorded only during that await must still count as gone. Add the probe as a named sequence that checks the restored note shows after one bump.
2. **minor**, `src/tests/lib/features/event-calendar/event-calendar.model.test.ts:472`: lifting the limit adds almost no coverage. Across the 300 seeds the generator fires an outside delete during a calendar restore 3 times, and only once on the restored note's name. With main's `event-calendar.service.ts` swapped in, all 10 random-run tests still pass, so only the named sequence pins the fix. Restores run by `undoEventChange` / `redoEventChange` (`restoreNow` at `event-calendar.service.ts:1240`) and the toast undo (`:1209`) are not counted in `run.restoring`, so an outside delete racing them is still excluded. **Fix:** count undo/redo/toast restores in `run.restoring` too. Consider raising the weight of `outsideDelete` while `run.restoring > 0`, or pairing it with `holdScan`, so the random runs reach the restore's tail.

## Resolution

1. **Fixed** in 28f81314. `restoreNow` keeps its `deletedOutside` set registered past `restoreItem`; when a recorded delete matches the restored path (or a folder above it) it awaits `exists(restored)` (an error counts as present), and the note is `gone` only when the disk agrees. The `exists` IPC runs only on a match, so a restore with no outside delete keeps its timing. Deletes recorded during that await stay in the set. The reviewer's probe is the named sequence "an outside delete of another note at the path, recorded while a calendar restore of it is in flight (Issue 49)" (`event-calendar.model.test.ts`). It failed before the fix (the store did not show Standup after one bump) and passes now.
2. **Fixed** in 8c10c9ff. `restores()` in the model test counts an undo of a `delete` op or a redo of a `create` op toward `run.restoring`, when no field write is in flight (one could record an op above the one the undo or redo takes). While a restore runs, the outside delete's weight goes from 2 to 12. Measured with a temporary counter (reverted): 32 outside deletes during a restore across the 300 seeds, up from 3. With main's `event-calendar.service.ts` swapped in, 5 of the 10 random-run tests fail (seeds 24, 33, ...: a clockless `create` overlay left over a deleted note), and all pass with the branch's service. The model has no toast-undo op. `undoDeleteEvent` (`service.ts:1209`) calls the same `restoreNow` as the `restore` op and differs only by `retire`, so the toast was not added to the model.
