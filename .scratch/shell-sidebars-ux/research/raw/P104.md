## Verdict

still_real: partial. The core problem holds: Properties, Backlinks and Outgoing links have no palette toggle and no header hide action, and their only switches are in Settings > Layout (`LayoutSection.svelte:127-151`). Partly wrong: `open(section)` has a production caller (`SearchPanel.svelte:190`), there is no existing "header menu" to extend (the header is `SidebarSection`, a plain `button` or `div` with no menu), and Table of contents already has a palette toggle (`commands.ts:330-336`), which is the pattern the proposal copies.
