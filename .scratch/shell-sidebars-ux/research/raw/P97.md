## Verdict

still real: partial. The dead end holds at HEAD: EditorView.svelte:156 is one line, and the right sidebar mounts with "No file open" for the no-tab state (AppShell.svelte:265 via layout.logic.ts:24 returning false for null). The proposed mechanism does not hold as written: `hidesRightSidebar(null)` is deliberately false today, and changing it also hides the AppShell toggle (AppShell.svelte:189) and makes toggleRightSidebar a no-op with no tab (layout.service.ts:31), and it flips the null contract asserted at layout.logic.test.ts:48-51. The hint itself is still valid; its shortcut labels can come from `shortcutFor` (global-keybindings.ts:211).
