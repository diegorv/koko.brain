In the `flex items-baseline gap-2` row, the folder span (`min-w-0 truncate`) keeps its full text width as its flex basis, so all three items shrink in proportion. The title then wraps and "2 of 3 open" breaks over two lines while the folder still shows most of its path. Probe (headless Chromium, same CSS, 360 px text column, which is the space at a pane of about 720 px with the list column, or at 560 to 600 px without it): title 158 px wide and 2 lines, count 41 px and 2 lines (28 px tall), folder 145 px. Before this change the folder had its own line, so the title and count had the whole width, which makes this a regression for long folder paths. With `flex: 1 1 0%` on the folder: title 1 line, count 1 line, folder truncated to 51 px. Fix: give the folder span `flex-1` (basis 0) next to `min-w-0 truncate` and the count `shrink-0`, so the folder takes only the leftover space. Add those classes to the assertions in `TasksView.filters.test.ts:220-229`, then do the 420/800 px by-eye check the Issue asks for.
