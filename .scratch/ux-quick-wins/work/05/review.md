Fix (optional): double-click a real card to open its textarea, switch tabs, and assert the textarea is still in the DOM.

The filter also clears when you switch to any other tab (the view unmounts) or toggle source mode. Fix (optional): "when you leave the board (another tab, source mode, or closing it)".
