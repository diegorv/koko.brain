## Findings

1. minor — `src/lib/plugins/calendar/CalendarNavigator.svelte:23` (and `src/tests/lib/plugins/calendar/CalendarNavigator.test.ts:146`). After a restart the mini month always opens on the current month (`calendarStore` starts at `new Date().getMonth()`). If the saved day is in another month, the note list shows "10 Sep 2026" while the navigator shows November with nothing picked. The probe above confirmed this. The "highlights the saved day after a remount (a restart)" test calls `showMonth(2026, 9)` before it mounts, so it passes despite the bug. Fix: when the navigator mounts (in `untrack`), show the month of the picked day. This derives the month from the pick, so it is not a saved calendar position. Then change the test to start from `calendarStore.reset()` with a saved day in an earlier month.
2. minor — `src/lib/core/layout/AppShell.svelte:117,210-219`. The new registered-navigator branch (`<registeredNavigator.component />` in the navigator pane, and the note list fed by `railItem.lens`) has no unit or E2E test. Types and Files each have `e2e/specs/rail-shell.spec.ts` specs ("opens the navigator", "New here ... the list shows it", "survives a restart"). So a regression in this glue code, the only place the Calendar navigator is mounted, would pass every test. Fix: add a Calendar spec to `rail-shell.spec.ts`: pick a day, the list shows its notes, New here adds a dated note to the list, and the pick survives a restart. It can be a follow-up, since the Issue has no E2E criterion.
3. nit — `src/lib/plugins/calendar/CalendarNavigator.svelte:52-54`. "Go to today" (the month label) saves today's key, so the pick is pinned to that date: after midnight the lens stays on yesterday. With no saved day, the lens follows the clock. Consider saving `railDay: null` there, so "today" keeps its moving meaning (the highlight is the same either way).

## Resolution

1. Fixed. `calendarStore.showMonth(year, month)` sets the shown month without touching the selected day; `CalendarNavigator` calls it once at mount (in `untrack`) when `layout.railDay` holds a valid saved day, so a restart opens on the saved day's month. With no saved day the store already starts on today's month, so it is left alone. The remount test now starts from `calendarStore.reset()` with the clock on 1 Nov 2026 and `railDay` 2026-09-10, and asserts "September 2026" plus the picked cell (it failed before the fix with "November 2026"). `calendar.store.test.ts` covers `showMonth`.
2. Fixed. `e2e/specs/rail-shell.spec.ts` "Calendar: a picked day lists its notes, New here adds a dated note, and the pick survives a restart": opens Calendar from the rail, steps to last month, picks the 15th, checks the label, the rule and that the list is exactly the dated note, runs New here (file content `date: <day>`, row listed), polls `layout.railDay`, restarts, and checks the rail item, the month label, the picked cell and the list. Passes via `bash scripts/e2e.sh`.
3. Fixed. "Go to today" now shows today's month and saves `railDay: null`, so the pick is today and follows the clock. The test checks `railDay` is null and that the picked cell moves from 3 Dec to 4 Dec across midnight.
