## Findings

1. minor - src/app.css:72 and src/app.css:113 - Every other `UIColors` token (48 of 58) has a static fallback in the `:root` and `.dark` blocks of app.css, but the ten new ones (`--type-*`, `--warning`) have none. Until `applyActiveTheme` runs after settings load (settings.service.ts:40), `color: var(--type-green)` is invalid at computed-value time, so the icon inherits the text color, and `text-warning` / `bg-type-*` utilities resolve to nothing. Fix: add the ten variables to both blocks, with the KokoBrain Default values in `.dark` and light equivalents in `:root`, as the other tokens do.
2. minor - src/lib/features/type-definitions/TypeNoteList.svelte:574 - The criterion names the notes' rows and their tabs, but only the Types sidebar row has a rendered test. Rows and tabs are covered only by composing icon-resolver.test.ts and IconRenderer.test.ts. A change to `resolveNoteIcon` or to the tab's color prop would not fail any test. Fix: add a TypeNoteList (or EditorTabs) mount test in which a note with `isA: Project` inherits `_color: green` and its row svg's `style.color` is `var(--type-green)`.
3. nit - src/lib/core/settings/theme-builder.logic.ts:242 - `typeGray` is `mutedFg`, and in muted themes it sits close to a hue: the probe found Graphite Violet `typeTeal` #92b0b4 vs `typeGray` #a8a8b0 (RGB distance 24) and Twilight Violet `typePurple` #9878B8 vs `typeGray` #908aa4 (28). Contrast passes everywhere, but nothing checks that the categories stay distinct, and nobody has looked at them in the app. Fix: check the palette by eye in those two themes, and add a gray override there if they read as one color.
