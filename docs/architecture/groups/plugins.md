src/lib/plugins holds the optional modules that sit above core/ and features/: the Calendar rail navigator, Graph View, Kanban boards, the vault mods host, 1:1 notes, periodic notes, QueryJS blocks, Quick Capture, the table of contents, the template picker and the word count widget. They depend inward on core/, features/ and utils/. The app composition root (src/lib/app) imports most of them through lifecycle registration, commands, keybindings, the editor host and the overlay and shell components, and the (app) route layout registers the Quick Capture listener. Where a lower layer needs a plugin surface, the plugin registers into a registry or store owned by a lower layer at vault open (the rail registry, the note action bar registry, the event-calendar daily note opener, core/rule-actions for mods), so core/, features/ and utils/ never import plugins/. Plugin-to-plugin imports are limited to an allowlist in scripts/import-graph.mjs: calendar, one-on-one and quick-capture may import periodic-notes, and mods may import queryjs, one-on-one and periodic-notes. Most plugins have no Rust code of their own and reach the backend through core services or the typed IPC wrapper. Quick Capture pairs with src-tauri/src/quick_capture, and mods use the read_mod_data and write_mod_data commands in src-tauri/src/commands/vault.rs.
